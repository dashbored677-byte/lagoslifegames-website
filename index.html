Frequently Asked Questions

Are there official Lagos Life codes?

There is no reliable evidence of active official Lagos Life codes unless the developers publish them in an official channel. Treat any code claim with caution and focus on in-game progression.

What is the best first job?

Beginners usually do best with jobs that balance pay, manageable shift length and simple skill requirements. A consistent starter role is often better than a difficult high-risk option.

How do I make money quickly?

The best approach is a stable routine: understand your needs, take a realistic job, improve money-saving habits and avoid overspending on rent while you build a buffer.
