# LagosLifeGames.online

Professional gaming guide website for Lagos Life browser game.

## Features

- Modern dark green, black and white gaming aesthetic
- Fully responsive mobile-first design
- 10 SEO-optimized guide pages
- Sticky mobile and desktop navigation
- Site-wide search functionality
- Clean, fast and lightweight
- Schema markup for SEO
- Accessibility-friendly

## Pages

1. Homepage
2. How to Play Lagos Life
3. Lagos Life Jobs & Salaries
4. How to Make Money in Lagos Life
5. Lagos Life Best Careers
6. Lagos Life Codes & Cheats
7. Lagos Life App
8. Lagos Life Tips & Tricks
9. Lagos Life Houses & Rent
10. Lagos Life Map & Locations
11. Lagos Life Beginner Guide

## Local Development

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open http://localhost:8000
